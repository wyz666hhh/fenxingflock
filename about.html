About Us

Who we are and why we do what we do

Foshan, Guangdong — the heart of China's manufacturing hub

We Know Flocking — Because We've Lived It

Foshan Fenxing Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in 2026 with a clear mission: to be the most reliable supplier of flocking raw materials for manufacturers around the world.

Our team brings years of hands-on experience from the flock fiber industry. Before starting this company, we worked directly in flock fiber sales — talking to factory owners, understanding their production challenges, and learning what really matters when sourcing raw materials.

That experience is what sets us apart. We don't just sell products — we understand your production line, your quality requirements, and your cost pressures.

Small Team, Big Commitment

We are a small company — and we see that as an advantage. Every inquiry gets direct attention from someone who understands the product. There's no bureaucracy, no delay, no passing your message through five different departments.

Our size also means we keep our costs low — and that savings goes to you in the form of competitive pricing. We don't have big overhead; we don't need big margins.

We are based in Foshan, Guangdong — a city at the center of China's textile and chemical manufacturing supply chain. This location gives us direct access to the best factories, the fastest logistics, and the latest industry developments.
