About Us

Who we are and why we do what we do

Foshan, Guangdong — the heart of China's manufacturing hub

We Know Flocking — From Years of Experience

Foshan Fenxing Materials Co., Ltd. was founded in 2026 with a clear mission: to be the most reliable supplier of flocking raw materials for manufacturers around the world.

Our team brings years of hands-on experience from the flock fiber industry. Before starting this company, we worked directly in flock fiber sales — speaking with factory owners, troubleshooting production challenges, and learning firsthand what really matters when sourcing raw materials.

That practical experience sets us apart. We don't just sell products — we understand your production line, your quality standards, and your cost pressures.

Small Team, Big Commitment

We're a lean team by design — and that's our advantage. Every inquiry gets direct attention from someone who understands the product. No bureaucracy, no delays, no passing your message through five different departments.

Being lean means lower overhead — and those savings are passed directly to you through competitive pricing. We keep our costs low so your costs stay low.

We are based in Foshan, Guangdong — in the heart of China's textile and chemical manufacturing supply chain. This location gives us direct access to the best factories, the fastest logistics, and the latest industry developments.
